# 10_interpretation.md
Stage 10 — Interpretation, Insight & Decisions

---

## Purpose
Convert validated outputs into **clear, decision‑ready recommendations** with explicit risk, confidence, and accountability.

This stage answers:
- What should we do?
- Why is this the right action?
- What could go wrong?
- Who owns the outcome?

If a decision owner cannot act after this stage, the work is incomplete.

---

## Primary Tool
- LLM (Claude / Copilot) for synthesis and narrative drafting

---

## Inputs
- Approved `07_validation.md`
- Production outputs from `08_production_notes.md`
- Delivery artifacts from `09_exec_narrative.md`
- Business context and operating constraints

---

## Interpretation & Decision Checklist

### 1. Synthesize Key Findings in Plain Language
Summarize:
- What changed
- What matters
- What does *not* matter

Use business language only.
Avoid:
- Model mechanics
- Statistical jargon
- Unnecessary precision

The goal is shared understanding, not technical completeness.

---

### 2. Restate the Decision Context
Explicitly restate:
- The decision being supported (from Stage 0)
- The primary KPI
- The time horizon

This prevents scope drift and reframes the narrative around action.

---

### 3. Explain Implications Over Time
Describe implications for:
- Near‑term decisions (next days / weeks)
- Medium‑term planning (quarterly horizons)

Highlight:
- Trade‑offs
- Irreversible vs reversible actions
- Timing sensitivity

Not all insights require immediate action.

---

### 4. Provide Prioritized Recommendations
List recommended actions in priority order.

For each recommendation, include:
- Expected impact (directional or quantified)
- Confidence level (high / medium / low)
- Dependencies or prerequisites

Avoid bundling multiple decisions together.

---

### 5. Explicitly Frame Risk and Uncertainty
State clearly:
- Key assumptions
- Known limitations
- Residual uncertainty
- Where the model or analysis is weakest

Risk framing builds trust more than confidence claims.

---

### 6. Define What Would Change the Recommendation
Document:
- What new data would change the conclusion
- What signals would trigger re‑evaluation
- What outcomes would invalidate assumptions

This turns insight into a living decision framework.

---

### 7. Assign Ownership and Accountability
For each decision or action:
- Name the owner
- Define the execution window
- Specify how success or failure will be evaluated

Insights without owners do not produce outcomes.

---

### 8. Define Next Validation Checkpoints
Specify:
- When results will be reviewed
- What metrics will be monitored
- Whether a re‑analysis or retraining is expected

This closes the loop between decision and learning.

---

### 9. Prepare the Executive Narrative Artifact
Produce a concise narrative that includes:
- Decision summary
- Recommended actions
- Expected upside and downside
- Confidence and risk framing
- Clear ask of leadership

This artifact should stand alone without verbal explanation.

---

## Required Outputs
- `10_executive_narrative.md` (decision‑ready memo)
- Decision‑ready slide draft (via Copilot 365)

---

## Exit Criteria
You may conclude the work only if:
- The decision owner understands the recommendation
- Upside and downside are explicit
- Risks and uncertainty are acknowledged
- Ownership and next steps are clear

✅ Stage 10 complete. Decision enabled.
