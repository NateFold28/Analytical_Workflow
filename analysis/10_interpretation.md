# Stage 10 — Interpretation, Insight & Decisions

## Purpose
Convert outputs into clear recommendations with explicit risk framing.

## Tool
- Claude (Desktop)

## Inputs
- Model outputs
- Business context
- Operating constraints

## Steps
1. Synthesize key findings in plain business language.
2. Explain implications for near-term and medium-term planning.
3. Provide prioritized decisions with expected impact.
4. State assumptions, limitations, and confidence level explicitly.
5. Assign owners, timelines, and next validation checkpoints.

## Required outputs
- `executive_narrative.md`
- Decision-ready slide draft (via Copilot 365)

## Exit criteria
- Decision owner can act with clear upside, downside, and accountability.
